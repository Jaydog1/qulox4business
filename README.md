# Qulox Business
A business social platform - for networking and communication
Qulox for Business is a platform full of professional tools, advertising options, and profile features designed to help companies, brands, and organizations create and connect, build public awareness, and drive sales on the platform. 

What is Qulox for Business? It is an ecosystem built for commercial entities rather than individual users. It transforms a standard social media presence into a proactive marketing, customer service, and market research hub.
Key Features & Capabilities Professional Profiles: Upgrade standard pages to display specific business categories, physical locations, operating hours, and direct contact buttons.Advanced Analytics: Access performance data to track impressions, engagement rates, audience demographics, and campaign reach.
Paid Advertising (Qulox Ads): 
Utilize targeted promotional campaigns, sponsored posts, and keyword or follower targeting to expand reach beyond organic followers.
Customer Support Integration: Handle real-time customer feedback, inquiries, and direct messaging support efficiently through dedicated agent workflows. Verification Badges: Secure official organizational checkmarks (such as gold checkmarks) to prove brand authenticity.
